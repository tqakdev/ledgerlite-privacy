LedgerLite Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

LedgerLite is a personal finance app built on a simple principle: your financial data belongs to you and stays on your device.

Data we collect

We collect nothing. LedgerLite has no servers, no account system, and no analytics. All data you enter — expenses, subscriptions, budgets, and categories — is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework.

Data we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone, address)
  • No financial account credentials
  • No advertising or tracking identifiers
  • No crash reports or usage analytics
  • No location data

Exchange rate requests

When you log an expense in a foreign currency, LedgerLite fetches live exchange rates from two free public APIs:

  • frankfurter.dev (primary)
  • open.er-api.com (fallback)

These are standard HTTPS requests containing only the currency codes you requested. No personal data is included. Rates are cached locally so repeat lookups do not require additional network calls.

iCloud

LedgerLite does not currently use iCloud sync. Data remains local to the device it was entered on.

Face ID / Touch ID

If you enable the biometric lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS (LocalAuthentication framework). LedgerLite never sees or stores your biometric data.

Third-party sharing

We do not sell, share, or transmit your data to any third party — ever.

Children

LedgerLite does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a new "Last updated" date.
