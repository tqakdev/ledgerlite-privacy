LedgerLite Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

LedgerLite stores all data — expenses, subscriptions, and categories — locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to us or any third party.

Exchange rates are fetched from frankfurter.dev and open.er-api.com when you log an expense in a foreign currency. These requests contain no personal information.

We do not collect analytics, crash reports, advertising identifiers, or any other data. We have no servers and no account system.

Questions: open an issue on the app's GitHub repository.
